# Vercelian: your Vercel deployments, live in the menu bar

Vercelian is a small, native Mac app that shows every build at a glance and tells you the moment one fails. No dashboard tabs, no refreshing.

Open source · macOS 14 or later · Mac App Store coming soon

[Download for Mac](https://github.com/deebov/vercelian/releases/latest/download/Vercelian.zip) · [View on GitHub](https://github.com/deebov/vercelian)

![Vercelian's popover under the macOS menu bar, listing queued, building, ready, and failed deployments](https://vercelian.co/assets/preview.png)

## What it does

- **Know the moment a build fails.** The triangle in your menu bar turns orange while your latest deploy builds and red when it fails, and a notification tells you which commit broke it.
- **Every detail, one click away.** See the branch, commit, repository, and environment of any deployment, and copy its URL.
- **Act without opening a browser.** Cancel a build or redeploy from its details, or swipe left for shortcuts to the site, its Vercel page, and its logs.
- **All your teams and projects.** Switch between your personal account and your teams, and filter by project.
- **Private by design.** Your access token stays in the macOS Keychain and is sent only to Vercel's API. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.

## Set up in a minute

1. **Download Vercelian and open it.** A triangle appears in your menu bar. GitHub builds aren't notarized, so the first time, allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
2. **Create an access token.** Click the triangle, then *Continue with Vercel*. Your browser opens your Vercel token page.
3. **Paste it and click Connect.** Your deployments appear right away and stay up to date.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
